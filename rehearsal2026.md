# Pentatonic Woodwinds Rehearsal Framework

## 0–5 min — Arrival & Individual Warm-Up

- Setup
- Personal long tones and (optional) chromatic scale
- Individual tuning
- Light articulation

Rehearsal begins formally at minute 5.

---

## 5–20 min — Chamber Warm-Up (15 Minutes Total)

### 1. Unison Tone Center (3 min)

- Sustain one pitch (rotating weekly)
- 8 counts
- Decrescendo to niente
- Re-enter together without conductor

Focus:
- Immediate pitch center
- Unified tone color
- Identical release

---

### 2. Scale of the Week (5 min)

- Full range (1–2 octaves as appropriate)
- ♩ = 72, legato
- Once at performance tempo
- Tune tonic chord carefully
- Resolve to dominant → return to tonic

Goal:
- Tone consistency
- Harmonic awareness
- Ensemble listening

---

### 3. Blend Exercise (4 min — choose one per rehearsal)

**Option A – Chorale Fragment**
- Use 4 measures from repertoire
- Slow tempo
- Focus on balance and shaping

**Option B – Dynamic Swell**
- Sustain chord pp → ff → pp
- Maintain pitch center and tone stability

**Option C – Staggered Entry**
- Enter same pitch 2 beats apart
- Match tone color precisely

---

### 4. Articulation Alignment (3 min)

- Take one measure from repertoire
- Play:
  - All slurred
  - All short
  - As written

Goal:
- Identical attack
- Unified length
- Clean releases

---

## 20–70 min — Repertoire Work

- Work in focused 10–15 minute sections
- Address specific issues
- Re-run immediately after corrections
- Keep discussion concise
- (Optional) 2-minute stretch reset if energy drops

---

## 70–85 min — Full Run-Through

- Perform a complete section without stopping
- Build performance mindset
- Maintain musical flow

---

## 85–90 min — Closing

- Brief reflection:
  - What improved?
  - What needs attention?
- Assign specific practice goals for next rehearsal
- End on a positive note

---

# Ensemble Captain Responsibilities

The Ensemble Captain:

- Leads scale of the week
- Initiates tuning process
- Counts off repertoire
- Helps maintain rehearsal focus
- Models professional preparation and behavior
