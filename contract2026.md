# Pentatonic Woodwinds Ensemble Agreement

## Our Purpose

We come together to:

- Grow as musicians  
- Prepare performances we are proud of  
- Support each other through focused and respectful collaboration
- Have fun!

This ensemble works best when everyone contributes equally in preparation, attention, and musical effort.

---

## Our Mission

The Pentatonic Woodwinds was formed in September 2024 with two missions: to share an appreciation for woodwinds in the Chinese community and to promote Chinese music and culture in the Omaha Metro area. This quintet, consisting of a flute, an oboe, and three clarinets, is dedicated to both arrangements of traditional Chinese music and modern compositions inspired by Chinese culture.

---

## Our Commitment

- We attend rehearsals consistently and arrive on time
- If we must miss rehearsal, we notify the group in advance
- We practice difficult passages between rehearsals so group time is productive
- Improvement matters more than perfection

---

## Preparation Culture

Before each rehearsal, we aim to:

- Practice difficult sections carefully and slowly
- Mark music with questions or problem spots
- Bring instrument, music, pencil, reeds, tuner, and other necessary materials
- Arrive ready to begin at the scheduled start time

---

## Rehearsal Culture

During rehearsal, we agree to:

- Give full attention when someone is playing or speaking
- Keep phones away unless needed for musical purposes
- Listen openly and respectfully when others receive feedback
- Offer suggestions constructively
- Mistakes are part of learning-no laughing at others

---

## Shared Leadership

This ensemble includes:

- **Organizer** – With feedbacks from all ensemble members, prepares music and performance schedule
- **Captain** – Helps lead warm-ups, tuning, and rehearsal flow

We agree to respect both roles and support each other in maintaining a focused and positive rehearsal environment

---

## Performances

When we perform:

- We represent ourselves and our families with professionalism
- We prepare thoroughly
- We support each other on stage

---

## If Challenges Arise

If someone is repeatedly unprepared or unfocused, we will first address it respectfully through conversation.  
The goal is always improvement and growth.
